# Original rewrite examples

These examples are original. They do not come from the standard.

Before: “Following the completion of the sample check, you can begin the next test.”

After: “After the sample check, you can start the next test.”

Before: “The cable might be loose, but the test does not establish the cause.”

After: “The cable may be loose. The test does not establish the cause.”

The second rewrite preserves uncertainty and the limit of the evidence. A shorter sentence alone does not prove STE compliance.
